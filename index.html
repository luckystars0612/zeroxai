01Research areas

AI for security

Autonomous and LLM-assisted agents for vulnerability discovery, reverse engineering, attack surface management, and triage.

Security of AI

Prompt injection, model and agent abuse, data and supply-chain risk, and robust guardrails for AI-driven systems.

Vulnerability research

Low-level analysis of binaries, drivers, and firmware, with an emphasis on coordinated disclosure.

Evaluation & verification

Reproducible methods to measure what AI security tools actually find, and to reject what they only claim.

02Approach

  1. HypothesizeFrame a precise, testable security question.
  2. BuildPrototype tooling and agents with strict scope controls.
  3. VerifyReplay every finding independently before it counts.
  4. DiscloseShare results responsibly with the people who can fix them.

03Principles

Responsible

Authorized targets only. Coordinated disclosure by default.

Evidence-first

No finding is trusted until it is reproduced.

Minimal by design

Small attack surface, in our tools and on this site.
